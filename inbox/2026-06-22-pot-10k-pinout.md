# 10K rotary potentiometer — verification note

Added `core:pot-10k` to `@protopulse/parts`. Fills a real gap — the seed library had no
variable-resistor/wiper topology. Tyler owns a "10K Ohm Rotary Potentiometer" (component
log line 2322).

## Part
- **MPN:** generic 10 kΩ rotary pot
- **Class:** `resistor` (variable resistor), refPrefix **RV** (potentiometer designator). 3 pins.
- Footprint deferred.

## Pinout — 3 terminals
- **A, B** — the two fixed ends of the 10 kΩ resistive track.
- **WIPER** (middle) — the adjustable tap.
- Used as a variable resistor (A↔WIPER) or a voltage divider (A↔WIPER↔B). ~300° rotation,
  linear taper assumed for general use.

## ERC modeling
- All 3 terminals → **passive** (a potentiometer is a passive resistive element).

## Verification
- The 3-terminal pot topology (two track ends + center wiper) is universal; confirmed
  against the component log's terminal description (T1 end, T2 wiper, T3 end).
