# P30N06LE power MOSFET — verification note

Added `core:p30n06le` to `@protopulse/parts`. Logic-level N-channel power MOSFET (Tyler
owns it — "P30N06LE Power MOSFET", WeiMeet-branded, in the component log). Distinct from
the small-signal SOT-23 AO3400 already in the seed. Pinout **web-verified**.

## Part
- **MPN:** RFP30N06LE (Fairchild / onsemi), TO-220AB
- **Class:** `transistor`, refPrefix **Q**. 3 pins. Footprint deferred.

## Pinout — TO-220AB
Pin **1 = Gate, 2 = Drain, 3 = Source** (label facing you, pins left→right). The metal tab
is internally connected to **Drain**. 60 V Vds, 30 A Id, Rds(on) 0.047 Ω, Vgs(th) 2–4 V —
logic-level (fully enhances from 5 V, so a GPIO can drive it directly for low-side switching).

## ERC modeling
- **G, D, S → passive** — a discrete 3-terminal semiconductor (matches the AO3400 model).
