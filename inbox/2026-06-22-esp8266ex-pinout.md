# ESP8266EX WiFi SoC (QFN-32) — verification note

Added `core:esp8266ex` to `@protopulse/parts`. The BARE Espressif WiFi SoC (Tyler owns it —
component log line 526) for custom-PCB designs, distinct from the NodeMCU/ESP-12 modules
already in the seed. Pinout **datasheet-verified** (read as an image).

## Part
- **MPN:** ESP8266EX (Espressif)
- **Package:** QFN-32, 5×5 mm, 0.5 mm pitch, center exposed thermal pad = pin 33 (GND).
- **Class:** `ic`, refPrefix **U**. 33 pins (32 signal + GND pad). Footprint deferred.

## Pinout — 33 pins (datasheet Table 2-1)
Datasheet PRIMARY names kept; GPIO alts in parentheses:
1 VDDA, 2 LNA(RF), 3 VDD3P3, 4 VDD3P3, 5 VDD_RTC, 6 TOUT(ADC), 7 CHIP_EN, 8 XPD_DCDC(GPIO16),
9 MTMS(GPIO14), 10 MTDI(GPIO12), 11 VDDPST, 12 MTCK(GPIO13), 13 MTDO(GPIO15), 14 GPIO2,
15 GPIO0, 16 GPIO4, 17 VDDPST, 18 SDIO_DATA_2(GPIO9), 19 SDIO_DATA_3(GPIO10), 20 SDIO_CMD(GPIO11),
21 SDIO_CLK(GPIO6), 22 SDIO_DATA_0(GPIO7), 23 SDIO_DATA_1(GPIO8), 24 GPIO5, 25 U0RXD(GPIO3),
26 U0TXD(GPIO1), 27 XTAL_OUT, 28 XTAL_IN, 29 VDDD, 30 VDDA, 31 RES12K, 32 EXT_RSTB, **33 GND** (pad).

## ERC modeling
- **VDD rails (VDDA ×2, VDD3P3 ×2, VDD_RTC, VDDPST ×2, VDDD) + GND pad → power_in.**
- **GPIO / JTAG (MTxx) / SDIO / UART I/O pins → bidi.**
- **TOUT (ADC), CHIP_EN, EXT_RSTB (reset), RES12K (12 kΩ bias) → input** (analog/enable/reset/bias).
- **XTAL_OUT → output, XTAL_IN → input** (datasheet lists both as I/O; modeled functionally).
- **LNA → passive** (RF antenna interface — connects to the antenna/matching network, no digital drive).
