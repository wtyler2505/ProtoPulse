# KY-038 sound sensor module — verification note

Added `core:ky038` to `@protopulse/parts`. Pinout **web-verified** — NOT taken from
Tyler's shared `docs/parts-components-ect.md`. **Opens the Tier-2 sensor-module set**
and establishes the canonical "comparator sensor module" pattern (VCC/GND/DO/AO) that
recurs across flame, IR-obstacle, soil-moisture, vibration, etc.

## Part
- **MPN:** KY-038 (electret microphone + LM393 comparator)
- **Reference:** https://sensorkit.joy-it.net/en/sensors/ky-038 (LM393 is the core IC)
- **Function:** detects sound; DO trips when level exceeds the onboard-pot threshold,
  AO is the continuous analog level.
- **Class:** modeled `class: 'ic'`, refPrefix `U`

## Pinout — verified signals
| Pin | Name | Role | Electrical type (ERC) |
|----:|------|------|-----------------------|
| 1 | VCC | 3.3–5 V | power_in |
| 2 | GND | ground | power_in |
| 3 | DO | digital threshold out (HIGH > setpoint) | output |
| 4 | AO | analog sound-level out | output |

## Header-order caveat
The KY-038 silk ORDER is board-revision-dependent (espboards shows +V, GND, DO, AO;
other revisions reorder). The pin **names/functions are fixed**; numbering follows the
espboards-verified order. Same modeling stance as the DS3231 / RTC modules.

## The comparator-module pattern
KY-038 is the template for a whole family of LM393-comparator sensor breakouts that
share VCC/GND/DO(/AO). Future Tier-2 parts (flame sensor, IR obstacle, soil moisture,
vibration) follow the same shape but each is web-verified individually — pin counts
differ (some 3-pin digital-only, some 4-pin with AO).
