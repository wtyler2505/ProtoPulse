# HC-SR501 PIR motion sensor — verification note

Added `core:hc-sr501` to `@protopulse/parts`. Classic 3-pin PIR motion module (Tyler owns
it — "HC-SR501 PIR Motion Sensor Module" in the component log). Pinout **diagram-verified**.

## Part
- **MPN:** HC-SR501 (BISS0001-based pyroelectric PIR)
- **Class:** `ic`, refPrefix **U** (module). 3 pins. Footprint deferred.

## Pinout — 3-pin header
`GND, OUT, VCC` (pin 1 = GND, 2 = OUT, 3 = VCC). The **middle pin is always OUT** (the
orientation-independent invariant). **Correction:** an earlier text-only model had
`VCC/OUT/GND`, taken from a components101 *text table* that was reversed from the actual
board pictures. The mandatory board-diagram-verification sweep (2026-06-22) read three
authoritative pinout diagrams **as images** — lastminuteengineers front-view diagram,
components101 board photo, componentsinfo back-side view — and all three show **GND/OUT/VCC**.
The two END pins (GND/VCC) are silk-swapped on some clones, so the per-board silkscreen is
ground truth; modeled the canonical GND/OUT/VCC order.

## Behavior / ERC modeling
- **VCC, GND → power_in.** 4.5–20 V supply (onboard regulator; 5 V typical from Arduino).
- **OUT → output.** Digital HIGH (~3.3 V TTL) when motion detected, LOW idle; module-driven.
- On-board: 2 trim pots (sensitivity + time delay) and an H/L retrigger jumper — not header
  pins, not modeled. ~7 m range, ~120° FoV, 30–60 s warm-up.
