## Pinout — TO-92, 3-lead
Orientation: flat face toward you, leads pointing down.

| Pin | Function | Electrical type (ERC) |
|----:|----------|-----------------------|
| 1 (left)   | +VS (supply, 2.7–5.5 V) | power_in |
| 2 (middle) | VOUT (analog output)    | output   |
| 3 (right)  | GND                     | power_in |

## Electrical specs
- Output scale factor: **10 mV/°C** (TMP36)
- Output at 25 °C: **750 mV**
- 500 mV offset → negative temps read as positive voltages
- Operating range: **−40 °C to +125 °C**
- Supply: 2.7–5.5 V; supply current ~50 µA typ.
- Transfer function: `T(°C) = (Vout_mV − 500) / 10`
