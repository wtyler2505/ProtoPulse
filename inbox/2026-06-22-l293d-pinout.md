# L293D motor driver IC — verification note

Added `core:l293d` to `@protopulse/parts`. Standard quadruple half-H driver IC (Tyler owns
the bare DIP-16 — "L293D Motor Driver IC" in the component log). Pinout **web-verified**
against the ST datasheet — a universal, unambiguous DIP-16 part.

## Part
- **MPN:** L293D (ST / TI), DIP-16
- **Class:** `ic`, refPrefix **U**. Footprint deferred (no DIP-16 helper in the seed yet).

## Pinout — DIP-16
1=1,2EN, 2=1A, 3=1Y, 4=GND, 5=GND, 6=2Y, 7=2A, 8=VCC2 (motor 4.5–36 V),
9=3,4EN, 10=3A, 11=3Y, 12=GND, 13=GND, 14=4Y, 15=4A, 16=VCC1 (logic 4.5–7 V).
600 mA/channel, internal flyback diodes. Modeled the enables as EN12/EN34.

## ERC modeling
- **Enables (EN12/EN34) + A inputs (1A–4A) → input** (MCU-driven).
- **Y outputs (1Y–4Y) → output** (drive the motor/load).
- **VCC1, VCC2, 4× GND → power_in.**
