ProtoPulse

A browser-based EDA & hardware-prototyping platform. Dark, technical, cyan.

Principles

  • Dark-first. The canvas is near-black (--color-background, hsl 225 20% 3%). Build on bg-background.
  • Cyan is identity. --color-brand / --color-primary = hsl(190 100% 43%). Brand cyan signals "this is ProtoPulse"; interactive emphasis lives on the same hue.
  • Sharp, not soft. Radius 0; chamfered corners for character.
  • Function has color. Hardware concepts carry dedicated semantics, so a schematic, a PCB, and a validation panel speak one language:
power · VCC rails signal · GPIO data · buses success warning error
