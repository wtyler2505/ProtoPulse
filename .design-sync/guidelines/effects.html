Effects — cyan edge glow

Depth in ProtoPulse comes from a faint primary-cyan glow on edges, not drop shadows. Two intensities, both driven by --color-primary via color-mix.

.edge-glow

Resting state — a 1px cyan halo. Used on active panels, selected rows, focused canvas regions.

.edge-glow-strong

Emphasis — an 8px bloom. Hover/live states, running simulations, energized nets.
